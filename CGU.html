Terms of use.

These terms define the conditions for using MOTSA, a platform for certifying and verifying digital document authenticity.

Purpose

MOTSA helps users generate and verify proof that a document matches a certified version.

Service

The service may include web verification, desktop certification, public verification links and document metadata storage.

User duties

  • Use MOTSA lawfully.
  • Do not attempt to falsify certificates or verification data.
  • Keep account credentials secure.

Limits

MOTSA verifies integrity against registered proofs. It does not guarantee the legal truthfulness of the document content itself.
