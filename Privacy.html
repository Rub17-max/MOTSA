Privacy · Security

Privacy-first document verification.

MOTSA is designed to prove document authenticity while minimizing data exposure. The file content stays local during verification.

Local processing

When you verify a document, its fingerprint is calculated in your browser or on your device. The document itself is not uploaded to MOTSA for verification.

Principle: MOTSA should know enough to verify authenticity, not more.

Data collected

  • Account information such as email and optional profile details.
  • Document metadata required for certification, including file name, source and verification token.
  • Technical logs required to operate and secure the service.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data according to applicable privacy laws.

Contact

For privacy requests: privacy@motsa.tech.
